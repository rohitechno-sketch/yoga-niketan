Welcome to Yoga Niketan

Yoga Niketan is a place where people come together to practise yoga, breathe more mindfully, and slow down in the company of others who share the same intention. Whether you are taking your first steps onto the mat or continuing a practice you already love, our doors are open to you.

Our sessions are designed to support physical wellbeing, encourage mindfulness, and help create a more peaceful connection between body and mind. We believe yoga is for everybody and every body, and we welcome each visitor with warmth, patience and respect.
