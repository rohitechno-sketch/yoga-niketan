About Yoga Niketan

Our story, our approach to practice, and the community we are building together in Bangui Nagar.

Our Story

Our Story

Yoga Niketan began with a simple intention: to offer a quiet, welcoming space where people in Bangui Nagar could practise yoga and reconnect with themselves. [ADD HISTORY / FOUNDING STORY].

We continue to be guided by the traditional roots of yoga while keeping our doors open and approachable for every visitor, regardless of age, background or experience.

Our Approach

Our Approach

Our practice is rooted in tradition and shaped by care for every practitioner.

Traditional Yoga

Practices rooted in traditional yogic teaching, adapted thoughtfully for today's practitioners.

Mindful Movement

Postures practised slowly and attentively, with awareness of the body at every step.

Breath Awareness

Breathing practices that help bring attention back to the present moment.

Meditation

Quiet sitting practices that support focus, calm and reflection.

Personal Wellbeing

An emphasis on sustainable, gentle progress rather than pressure or comparison.

A Welcoming Community

A space where beginners and experienced practitioners can practise side by side.

Our Community

Our Community

Yoga Niketan is a welcoming space for beginners taking their first class and for experienced practitioners deepening a long-standing practice. Everyone is invited to move at their own pace, ask questions, and feel at home.

We believe that yoga is best practised together — in a community that supports, encourages and respects one another.
